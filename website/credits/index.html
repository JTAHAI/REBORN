The car at the heart of it

Buttercup was Justin Tahai’s customized 1999½ Mk IV Jetta. As Justin wrote: “I drove my son home for the first time in that car.” This is her second life on the road.

Project

Creator, owner, and tribute: Justin Tahai
Development: Justin Tahai’s AI-assisted 99½ REBORN project
Playable build: Build 009 / North Berwick driver-eye reconstruction
This package: Buttercup memorial website, opening dedication, credits, and open-source release materials.

Artwork and the real game

The promotional car and harbor images were generated earlier in this project and are reused here as concept art. They are not original photographs, verified records of the car’s exact trim, or captures of the current game. The image labeled “Actual Build 008 capture” is a runtime screenshot supplied with Build 008.

The playable build combines a credited third-party Mk IV model with project-authored geometry, rendering, and synthesized audio. This website adds no third-party web fonts, music, remote scripts, or new generated concept images.

Influences, not affiliations

DRIVER, Twisted Metal, Wacky Wheels, and Viper inspired the creative direction. Their names, characters, assets, and trademarks remain with their respective owners. This is an independent tribute, not an authorized installment of those properties or an official Volkswagen game.

North Berwick reconstruction references

The Build 009 hometown pass uses public Maine road/building data and openly licensed Wikimedia Commons photographs for selected landmark facades. Town Hall, Hurd Manor and the Olde Woolen Mill use attributed facade-atlas material; exact authors, licenses and source pages are retained in the facade-atlas manifest and world credits.

No Google Street View imagery is bundled in this release. Procedural homes and unreferenced landmarks remain visual game reconstructions rather than survey-accurate digital twins.
