A CELEBRATION OF LIFE / JUSTIN TAHAI

In loving memory..... the love of my life.

Yes. The car.

Those who have known me for a very long time, know that my beloved "Buttercup" was with me for a very long time. For 11 or 12 years, I enjoyed driving that car.

I drove my son home for the first time in that car.

Some cars are just transportation. Buttercup was never just a car to me.

And yes, I absolutely had named it after the horse in "Half-Baked." Get over it.

I ask not for condolences, flowers, or recognition. In a celebration of life for my beloved, I ask that others try this free-to-play game.

Instead of a moment of silence, take her for a drive.

It is my hope that the joy Buttercup brought me for so many years may now also be enjoyed by others.

She deserves more than a photograph to be remembered by. She deserves a road.

Introducing: 99-1/2 REBORN.

You don’t have to know her history or understand why somebody would miss an old Volkswagen this much.

In the style of the video game DRIVER, please enjoy the ride.

This one’s for you, Buttercup. We’ve still got some miles left.

01

Changing Maine roads

Rain builds standing water; cold turns moisture to ice. Snow packs, melts into slush and is cleared on fictional public-works schedules. Worn tires and brakes make the difference.

Select Seasonal Journey or a weather scenario in Settings.
02

A Jetta with a history

Fuel, component condition, faults, repairs and originality persist. Diagnose symptoms, preserve a part or replace it, and keep a record of the miles.

J opens your Jetta hub: status, workshop, town and journal.
03

A town that carries on

31 fictional incident definitions, bounded local traffic and roadside decisions build trust and leave consequences. Free Drive is not a race grid.

F interacts nearby · E retains the existing pulse action.
04

Find your own way

Search and pin North Berwick landmarks, pan and zoom the map, and remember slippery roads you actually drove. Four-way touch input keeps throttle under your control.

M or tap the minimap pauses and expands it. W / ↑ drives.
