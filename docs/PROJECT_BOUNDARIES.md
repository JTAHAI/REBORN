# REBORN project boundary

99½ REBORN and Carpooling to Hell's Arcade / Procedural Grand Prix are separate games.

REBORN's authoritative scope is:

- the memorial story and established four activities;
- North Berwick, Maine as the canonical Free Drive world;
- the owner-correct gloss-black 1999½ Mk IV Jetta, fitted sunroof, Maine plate `9855 MD`, and verified wheel/tire fitment;
- keyboard-equivalent mobile controls, paused map behavior, and local/static deployment;
- continued graphics, physics, town reconstruction, audio, accessibility, performance, and gameplay improvements designed specifically for REBORN.

Do not import Grand Prix branding, narrative, race-grid identity, named rivals, parody material, alternate vehicles, course progression, networking UI, or product terminology into REBORN. Generic engineering ideas may be rebuilt independently only when they serve REBORN on its own terms and are explicitly requested.
