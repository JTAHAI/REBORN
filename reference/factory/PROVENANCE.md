The owner photos supplied in this task remain external, reference-only material. The custom deep front fascia, lowered ride height, silver mirrors, wheel style, and `9855 MD` plate are inferred only from those owner photos; no claim is made that the production Mk IV used these parts.

## Model search decision

The exact-make listing located at Free3D (Volkswagen Bora/Jetta Mk4) is a $10 Editorial Only OBJ, with no UV map and no game-ready designation. It was not acquired, imported, or used. Any paid/licensed 3D acquisition needs user approval and a compatibility review before integration.
